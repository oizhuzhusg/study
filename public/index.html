Guide

How to Use Chem Coach

1. Choose the right profile

Use Student for the real learner and Tester for experiments. Each profile has separate saved mastery and practice history.

2. Pick a knowledge point

Use Knowledge Points on the left to practise Sec 1 foundations, Sec 2 Chemistry II, or one focused sub-skill.

3. Learn, answer, and submit

Read the concept and example, answer the current question, then submit for grading. Typed answers and photo uploads are both supported.

4. Follow the mastery lights

Green means mastered, yellow means practising, and red means the skill needs more work. The next question is chosen from weak areas.

5. Keep versions fresh

If a new version is available, an update banner appears. Click Update to reload the newest app without changing saved learner progress.
